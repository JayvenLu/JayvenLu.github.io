About

A full research pipeline, built as one system.

I work across the full development cycle of vision-based tactile sensors: compliant structure design and fabrication, imaging hardware, geometric and force calibration, 3D reconstruction, three-axis force estimation, and FPGA acceleration.

I am particularly interested in high-fidelity, high-speed tactile sensing for contact-rich robotic manipulation, where sensing mechanics, computational models, and deployment hardware must be designed together.

Current Direction

RGB-NIR geometry, force perception, and high-speed tactile computation.

My current work investigates curved vision-tactile sensing with complementary visible and near-infrared observations, boundary-aware depth reconstruction, three-axis force perception, and efficient image-to-perception pipelines.
