Hi there!

I am an M.Eng. student in Mechanical Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Hua Yang, with expected graduation in June 2027. My research focuses on vision-based tactile sensing and visuotactile learning for dexterous, contact-rich robotic manipulation.

My work spans tactile sensor design, 3D geometry reconstruction, three-axis force estimation, and real-time FPGA deployment. I also work on visuotactile fusion, imitation learning, and simulation-to-real transfer for multi-scale fine manipulation, connecting contact perception with robot action learning.

2024.03 - Present Research

FasTac: High-Speed Tactile Geometry and Force Perception

Led the development of a compact curved RGB-NIR tactile sensing system spanning sensor design, synchronized multispectral imaging, boundary-prior 3D reconstruction, HyperForce three-axis force estimation, and FPGA edge deployment.

Implemented hardware-oriented pipelines for surface-normal inference, boundary-prior depth reconstruction, and normal-force estimation on Xilinx Zynq UltraScale+ platforms.

Depth mean absolute error is 0.0415 mm. The FPGA image-to-normal-force pipeline achieves 1.09 ms/frame latency and 8.41 mJ/frame energy consumption. Computation rates reach 1022 Hz for image-to-depth and 918 Hz for image-to-normal-force.

Project page arXiv
2025.03 - 2025.12 Research

Visuotactile Imitation Learning for Multi-Scale Fine Manipulation

Worked on visuotactile imitation learning for multi-scale, contact-rich bimanual manipulation. The project combines tactile-informed simulation data generation, memory-efficient multimodal sparse attention, and multi-expert action learning to address limited demonstrations, small-target perception, and coarse-to-fine action coordination.

Reduces memory usage for multi-scale fusion from 24 GB to 7.4 GB. The complete method achieves a 100% success rate in the evaluated 800-step simulated long-horizon embroidery task.

2025 Research

Neural Inverse Rendering and Synthetic Tactile Data

Developed SMF-PSNN for near-field multi-source illumination self-calibration, surface-normal and depth reconstruction, and RGB-NIR tactile image synthesis. The calibrated rendering model generates transferable tactile observations for pose estimation, fingerprint recognition, and slip detection.

Neural inverse rendering reaches 30.22 dB PSNR, improving by 6.33 dB over the single-light-source baseline.
